Terms of Service

The terms under which I provide gardening services.

Last updated: January 2026

These terms of service govern the provision of gardening services by Gary Hall Garden Services ("I", "me", "my") to you ("the client"). By booking my services, you agree to these terms.

1. Quotes and Estimates

All quotes are free and provided without obligation. Quotes are based on the information available at the time of the site visit. If the scope of work changes after the quote is issued, I will discuss any adjustment with you before proceeding.

2. Booking and Scheduling

Bookings are confirmed once a date and time have been agreed. I aim to arrive on time and complete work as scheduled. In the event of bad weather or unforeseen circumstances, I will contact you as soon as possible to rearrange.

3. Payment

Payment is due on completion of work unless otherwise agreed in writing. I accept bank transfer and cash. For larger projects, a deposit may be required before work begins.

4. Cancellations

If you need to cancel or reschedule, please give me as much notice as possible. Regular maintenance clients are asked to provide at least 24 hours' notice where possible.

5. Access to Your Garden

Please ensure I have safe and reasonable access to your garden on the agreed day. If access is not possible and I am unable to carry out the work, a charge may apply to cover my time.

6. Green Waste

All green waste created during the job is taken away and disposed of responsibly, and is included in the quote unless agreed otherwise. If you prefer to keep the green waste for composting, please let me know in advance.

7. Liability

I am fully insured for the work I carry out. However, I cannot accept liability for:

  • Pre-existing damage to plants, trees, or structures
  • Hidden pipes, cables, or utilities not disclosed before work begins
  • Damage caused by weather or events outside my control
  • Plants that die or fail to thrive due to conditions beyond my control

Any concerns must be raised within 7 days of the work being carried out.

8. Plant Health and Results

While I take great care in all work, I cannot guarantee the survival or future health of plants, trees, or lawns. Horticulture is affected by many factors including weather, soil conditions, disease, and ongoing care.

9. Safety

I use professional equipment and follow safe working practices at all times. Please ensure children and pets are kept away from the working area while I am on site.

10. Photography

I may occasionally take before-and-after photographs of completed work for my portfolio and social media. I will never include identifying details such as house numbers or street names. If you would prefer your garden not to be photographed, please let me know.

11. Complaints

If you are not satisfied with any aspect of the work, please contact me directly at garyhallgardenservices@gmail.com or on 07951 434795. I will do my best to resolve any issue promptly and fairly.

12. Changes to These Terms

I may update these terms from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

If you have any questions about these terms, please email garyhallgardenservices@gmail.com.
