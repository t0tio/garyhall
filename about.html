My Story

From Passion to Profession

Gary Hall Garden Services is run by Gary Hall, a Hove-based gardener with over 10 years experience in horticulture and lawn care. Five years ago, I decided it was finally time to start my own business and now you can find me most days doing what I love, transforming gardens.

I provide a reliable, professional, and friendly service. Whether it's topiary, hedge trimming, lawn care, or a full garden transformation, I treat every garden with the care and attention it deserves.

Based in Brighton and Hove, I serve the local community with pride. I'm fully insured and offer free, no-obligation quotes. Follow me on social media to see my latest transformations!
