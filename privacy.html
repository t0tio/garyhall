Privacy Policy

How I collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last updated: January 2026

This privacy policy explains how Gary Hall Garden Services ("I", "me", "my") collects, uses, and protects any personal information you provide when you use this website or contact me for gardening services.

1. Who I Am

Gary Hall Garden Services is a sole-trader gardening business based in Brighton and Hove, United Kingdom. I am the data controller for any personal information collected through this website.

Contact: garyhallgardenservices@gmail.com · 07951 434795

2. Information I Collect

When you contact me through the website form, by phone, by email, or via WhatsApp, I may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number (if provided)
  • The service you are interested in
  • Your message and any details about your garden or property

I do not collect payment information through this website. Any payments for services are handled separately and securely.

3. How I Use Your Information

I use your information to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and provide a quote
  • Arrange and carry out gardening services
  • Keep records for accounting and legal purposes
  • Contact you about your job or future bookings

I will never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under UK GDPR, I process your personal data on the following bases:

  • Consent — when you submit the contact form and tick the consent box
  • Contract — when you engage me to carry out gardening work
  • Legitimate interest — to respond to enquiries and maintain business records

5. How Long I Keep Your Information

I keep enquiry information for up to 12 months. If you become a customer, I keep job records for up to 6 years for accounting and legal reasons, after which they are securely deleted.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal data I hold about you
  • Ask me to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask me to delete your information (subject to legal requirements)
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)

To exercise any of these rights, contact me at garyhallgardenservices@gmail.com.

7. Cookies

This website does not currently use tracking cookies. Third-party services embedded on this site (such as Google Maps and Google Fonts) may set their own cookies. Please refer to those providers' privacy policies for more information.

8. Data Security

I take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. However, no transmission over the internet is completely secure, and I cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

I may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how your data is handled, please email garyhallgardenservices@gmail.com.
